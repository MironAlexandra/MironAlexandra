# Hi, I'm Alexandra 👋

I'm a data & AI engineer based in Málaga, Spain 🇪🇸, working with teams in Antwerp and across Europe. My favourite moment in any project is when a messy, copy-paste-into-Excel process turns into something that just runs on its own.

Most of my days are a mix of code and people: figuring out what a team really needs, building it, and making sure they can keep using it once I've handed it over.

---

## 🛠️ What I build

- 🤖 **AI agents & assistants**: Claude skills and MCP connectors, chatbots that answer from your own documents (with sources), multi-agent setups with LangGraph
- 🧩 **Internal tools**: HR, finance and CRM-style apps that replace the spreadsheet nobody wants to touch
- 📈 **Growth setups**: Meta ads, Klaviyo email flows, Shopify landing pages and tracking you can actually trust
- 📊 **Dashboards & reporting**: from five messy exports to one clean Power BI or Looker Studio report a team refreshes themselves
- 🔮 **Predictive models**: product and customer scoring, similarity search, churn and forecasting, with explanations people can read
- 🎓 **AI workshops**: hands-on sessions that teach teams to use Claude and good prompts on their own work

- 🗣️ [**Multi-Agent Debate System**](https://github.com/MironAlexandra/Multi-AI-Agent-Debate-System-): four AI agents (a moderator, two debaters and a judge) argue it out with LangGraph
